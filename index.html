Mechanism

Three steps, every trade

The sequence runs inside the trade itself. Nothing here waits on you.

01 — Withhold

Someone buys or sells

A $PairexYd buy or sell hits the pair. The contract withholds a share of that trade before it settles.

02 — Acquire

The share buys $Pairex

The withheld amount is swapped for $Pairex and handed to the distributor, which holds it for the register of holders.

03 — Credit

Holders are credited

Every address is credited pro rata by balance. The credit is already yours — there is no claim to sign.
